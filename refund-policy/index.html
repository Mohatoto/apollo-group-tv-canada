    Refund Policy

    Last updated: December 2024

    1. Refund Eligibility

    At Apollo Group TV Canada, we want you to be completely satisfied with our service. If you are not satisfied with your subscription, you may request a refund within 24 hours of your initial purchase.

    2. Refund Process

    To request a refund, please contact our support team at support@apollogrouptv.ca with your order details. Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days.

    3. Non-Refundable Items

    After the 24-hour period, subscriptions are non-refundable. Partial refunds for unused portions of a subscription are not available. Promotional or discounted subscriptions may have different refund terms.

    4. Subscription Cancellation

    You may cancel your subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, you will continue to have access to the service until the end of your current billing period. No refunds will be provided for the remaining subscription period.

    5. Technical Issues

    If you experience technical issues that prevent you from using our service, please contact our support team. We will work to resolve the issue or provide appropriate compensation on a case-by-case basis.

    6. Contact Us

    For any refund-related inquiries, please contact us at support@apollogrouptv.ca
