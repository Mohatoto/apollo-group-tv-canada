Using One Account on Up to 5 Devices

By Apollo Group TV Canada · Last updated November 23, 2026

Apollo Group TV Canada plans can be used on up to 5 devices per account. That lets a household watch on a living-room TV, a bedroom TV, a tablet and a couple of phones under one subscription.

What the limit covers

The plans section of the website lists use on up to 5 devices per account for every plan length. The device types named on the site are smart TVs, phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.

If you need all five screens playing at the same moment, confirm that with support before you order, so the plan matches how your household watches.

How much internet speed five screens need

Every stream that plays at the same time needs its own share of your connection. The site lists 10 Mbps or more for an HD stream and 25 Mbps or more for a 4K stream.

Screens playing at once In HD With one of them in 4K
2 20 Mbps 35 Mbps
3 30 Mbps 45 Mbps
5 50 Mbps 65 Mbps

Leave some room for everything else on your network, such as video calls, downloads and smart-home devices.

Tips for a multi-screen household

  • Use a cable for the main TV. An Ethernet cable frees up Wi-Fi for the phones and tablets.
  • Put the router in the middle of the home. Rooms far from the router get the weakest signal.
  • Give the main TV priority when the house is busy. Many routers have a QoS or device priority setting, and our guide to router settings for streaming shows where to find it.
  • Watch in HD on small screens. A phone or tablet shows little benefit from 4K and uses far less speed in HD.
  • Keep your login details private. Sharing them outside your household can leave you without a free device when you want to watch.

Frequently asked questions

Do all five devices have to be in the same home?

The site says you can keep watching while travelling. If members of your household are regularly in different places, ask support how the device limit applies to you.

Does each device need its own subscription?

No. One account covers up to 5 devices.

What happens if my internet is too slow for every screen?

The streams compete for speed. You will usually see lower picture quality or pauses on one or more screens until someone stops watching.
