How Much Data Does Streaming TV Use?

By Apollo Group TV Canada · Last updated November 23, 2026

As a rough guide, an hour of HD streaming uses up to about 3 GB of data and an hour of 4K uses up to about 7 GB. A household that watches a few hours a day can use several hundred gigabytes in a month, so check whether your internet plan has a data limit.

Data use per hour

Apollo Group TV Canada does not publish data figures, so the table below uses the figures Netflix publishes for its own service as a reference. Real use varies by service, by programme and by how much is happening on screen.

Picture quality Data per hour (Netflix's published figures)
Standard definition up to 1 GB
HD up to 3 GB
4K (Ultra HD) up to 7 GB

What that adds up to in a month

Viewing per day In HD In 4K
2 hours about 180 GB about 420 GB
4 hours about 360 GB about 840 GB
6 hours about 540 GB about 1,260 GB

These totals are for one screen. If two screens play at the same time, the data use doubles.

How to check your own plan

  1. Look at your internet bill or your provider's app for a monthly data allowance. Many Canadian home plans are unlimited, but not all.
  2. Check your current usage in the same place. Most providers show the month so far.
  3. Ask what happens if you go over. Some providers charge per extra gigabyte, and some slow the connection.

Ways to use less data

  • Watch in HD on smaller screens. The difference from 4K is hard to see on a phone, tablet or small TV.
  • Stop playback when you leave the room. Live TV keeps using data for as long as it plays.
  • Switch off other heavy users such as cloud backups and game downloads on days when you are near your limit.

Streaming on mobile data

Mobile plans usually have much smaller allowances than home internet. An hour of HD can use a large share of a monthly phone plan, so use Wi-Fi where you can and check your phone plan before streaming on the go.

Frequently asked questions

Does live TV use more data than a movie?

Not by itself. Data use depends on picture quality and time watched. Live TV often adds up to more because it is left playing for longer.

Does pausing stop data use?

Pausing an on-demand title stops it shortly after the app has loaded a little ahead. Closing the app stops it completely.

Is an unlimited internet plan worth it for streaming?

If your household streams several hours a day, or watches in 4K, an unlimited plan removes the risk of extra charges.
