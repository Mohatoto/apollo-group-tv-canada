You can keep watching away from home as long as you have a device you are signed in on and a connection fast enough for video. Hotel Wi-Fi and mobile data are the two things most likely to get in the way, so test them before you settle in.

## What you need on the road

- **A device with the app set up.** Sign in and test it at home before you leave.
- **A connection of 10 Mbps or more for HD**, as listed by Apollo Group TV Canada. 4K needs 25 Mbps or more, which shared networks rarely provide.
- **Your login details**, kept somewhere safe in case you need to sign in again.

## Hotel and public Wi-Fi

Shared Wi-Fi is the most common cause of trouble. Many guests use the same connection, so the speed drops in the evening.

1. Run a speed test in your room before you start watching.
2. If the speed is low, ask the front desk whether a faster tier or a wired connection is available.
3. Watch in HD and avoid 4K.
4. Some hotel networks require a sign-in page. A phone or laptop handles that easily. A streaming stick may need the hotel's help to connect.

## Using mobile data

Mobile data works well when the signal is strong, but video uses it quickly. As a reference, Netflix states that HD streaming on its service uses up to 3 GB an hour. Check your phone plan's allowance, and check roaming charges before streaming outside Canada.

Using your phone as a hotspot for a laptop or tablet uses your mobile data in the same way.

## The device limit when you travel

Apollo Group TV Canada plans can be used on up to 5 devices per account. A phone or tablet you take with you counts as one of them, alongside the devices at home.

## If it does not work where you are

The website says you can stay connected wherever you go. Networks differ, though, and some workplaces, hotels and countries restrict video traffic. If the app will not play on a connection that passes a speed test, try a different network such as mobile data, then write to support@apollogrouptv.ca with where you are and what you see.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I watch outside Canada?

The site says the service lets you stay connected wherever you go. Results depend on the local network, so test it early in your trip and contact support if it does not play.

### Will my family at home still be able to watch while I travel?

Yes, within the limit of up to 5 devices per account.

### Why is the picture worse in a hotel than at home?

Hotel Wi-Fi is shared between many rooms. The app lowers picture quality to keep playing when less speed is available.
