---
title: Cutting Cable in Canada: What to Check First
description: A practical checklist for moving from cable TV to streaming in Canada, covering internet speed, data limits, devices, contracts and costs.
---
Before you cancel cable, check five things: your internet speed, your data allowance, your devices, your current contract, and whether the programmes you watch are available on the service you are moving to. Testing a streaming service for a month before you cancel is the safest order.

## 1. Check your internet speed

Streaming replaces the cable line with your internet connection. Apollo Group TV Canada lists 10 Mbps or more for each HD stream and 25 Mbps or more for each 4K stream. Add up the screens your household uses at the same time, then run a speed test near your main TV in the evening.

## 2. Check your data allowance

Some Canadian internet plans have a monthly data limit. Video uses a lot of data. As a reference point, Netflix states that its HD setting uses up to 3 GB an hour and 4K up to 7 GB an hour. A household that watches four hours of HD a day could use around 360 GB a month. If your plan has a limit, compare it with your viewing or ask about an unlimited plan.

## 3. Check your devices

Make a list of the screens you watch on and confirm each one is supported. The Apollo Group TV Canada website names smart TVs, phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. An older TV without apps usually needs a streaming stick or box.

## 4. Check your cable contract

- Look for an end date and any early-cancellation fee.
- Ask what equipment has to be returned, and by when.
- If your internet and TV are bundled, ask what the internet alone will cost. A bundle discount can disappear when you drop TV.

## 5. Check that your programmes are covered

Write down the channels and programmes your household would miss most. Ask the provider's support team whether they are included before you pay, and keep the answer. Do not rely on a general description such as "live sports" if one specific team or league is the reason you subscribe.

## 6. Run both for a month

Keep cable for a few weeks while you try the streaming service on your own internet connection and devices. A 30-day plan is designed for this. Cancel cable once you are sure the new setup works on a busy evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will I save money by cutting cable?

It depends on your current bill, on what your internet costs once it is no longer bundled, and on whether you need a faster or unlimited internet plan. Add those up before you decide.

### Do I need to tell my cable company in advance?

Usually yes. Check your agreement for the notice period and for how equipment must be returned.

### Can I keep my home phone and internet if I cancel TV?

In most cases yes, but the price can change when a bundle is broken. Ask your provider for the new monthly total.
