IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It delivers live channels and on-demand video through your internet connection, to an app on a device you already own, instead of through a cable line or a satellite dish.

## How IPTV works

A traditional cable or satellite service sends every channel to your home at once, and a set-top box picks the one you want. IPTV works the other way round. When you choose a channel or a movie, the app asks a server for that one stream, and the video travels to you over the internet in small pieces that play as they arrive.

Because the video arrives over the internet, the picture quality depends on your connection. A faster and steadier connection gives a sharper picture with fewer pauses.

## What you can watch with IPTV

Most IPTV services offer some mix of three things:

- **Live TV:** channels that play in real time, such as news, sports and entertainment.
- **Video on demand:** a library of movies and series you start whenever you like.
- **Catch-up or playback controls:** pausing, rewinding or restarting a programme, where the service supports it.

What is included differs from one provider to another, so check the plan details before you subscribe.

## What you need to use IPTV

1. **A device with a screen.** A smart TV, streaming stick, phone, tablet, laptop or desktop computer.
2. **An internet connection.** Apollo Group TV Canada lists 10 Mbps or more for HD and 25 Mbps or more for 4K.
3. **A subscription and login details.** These come from your provider after you sign up.

You do not need a cable outlet, a satellite dish or a rented set-top box.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is IPTV the same as a streaming app?

Both send video over the internet. The term IPTV is usually used for services built around live TV channels, while streaming apps are usually built around an on-demand library. Many services now offer both.

### Do I need a special box for IPTV?

No. Most services run as an app on a smart TV, streaming stick, phone, tablet or computer. Your provider tells you which devices are supported.

### Does IPTV use my internet data?

Yes. Every hour of video counts toward your internet plan's data allowance, and higher picture quality uses more. See our guide to [how much data streaming uses](/guides/streaming-data-usage/).
