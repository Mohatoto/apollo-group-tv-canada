You order Apollo Group TV Canada by email or through the order form, you pay by Interac e-Transfer, and your login details are sent after the payment is confirmed. The website does not take card payments.

## The steps from order to login

1. **Choose a plan.** Open the pricing section of the website and pick a plan length.
2. **Send your order.** The Subscribe button opens an email to apollogrouptvca@gmail.com with the plan filled in, or opens the order form. Add your name and phone number.
3. **Wait for the reply.** You receive payment instructions for your order.
4. **Send the Interac e-Transfer** from your own online banking, for the exact plan price, following the instructions you received.
5. **Receive your login details** once the payment is confirmed.

## How Interac e-Transfer works

Interac e-Transfer sends money from your bank account to another person or business in Canada, using their email address or phone number. You send it from your bank's website or app, so your banking details stay with your bank.

Two facts matter before you send one:

- **A deposited transfer cannot be reversed.** Interac states that once a deposit has been made there is no way to reverse the transaction.
- **Autodeposit removes the chance to cancel.** If the recipient uses Autodeposit, the money goes straight into their account, and several Canadian banks state that the transfer then cannot be cancelled.

## What to check before you send

- **The instructions came in reply to your own order.** Use only the payment details sent to you in that reply.
- **The amount matches the plan price** shown on the website.
- **You have read the refund policy.** Because the transfer itself cannot be reversed, a refund is something you request from support under that policy.
- **You keep the confirmation.** Save the confirmation number your bank gives you, and include it if you contact support.

If you are new to the service, starting with the 30-day plan keeps the amount small while you check that everything works on your devices.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay by card?

No. The website does not process card payments. Payment is by Interac e-Transfer.

### How soon do I get access after paying?

The site says most users are watching shortly after payment. If you have not received your login details, write to support@apollogrouptv.ca with your payment confirmation number.

### What if I sent the wrong amount?

Contact support with your confirmation number. If the transfer has not been deposited yet, your bank may also let you cancel it from your online banking.
