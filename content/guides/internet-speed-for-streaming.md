For Apollo Group TV Canada, plan on at least 10 Mbps of download speed for each HD stream and 25 Mbps for each 4K stream. If several people watch at once, add the numbers together.

## Speed needed per stream

| Picture quality | Apollo Group TV Canada lists | For comparison, Netflix recommends |
|---|---|---|
| HD (1080p) | 10 Mbps or more | 5 Mbps or higher |
| 4K (Ultra HD) | 25 Mbps or more | 15 Mbps or higher |

Different services publish different figures because they compress video differently. Live TV is also harder to compress than a movie, since it has to be prepared in real time. Using the higher figure gives you room to spare.

## Add it up for your household

Speed is shared between everything using your connection at the same moment. Count the streams you expect at your busiest time, then add a margin for phones, video calls and downloads.

| What is playing at once | Speed to plan for |
|---|---|
| 1 HD stream | 10 Mbps |
| 2 HD streams | 20 Mbps |
| 1 4K stream and 1 HD stream | 35 Mbps |
| 5 HD streams (the device limit) | 50 Mbps |

A sensible rule is to keep about a quarter of your connection free. If your streams add up to 30 Mbps, a 40 Mbps plan is a comfortable fit.

## How to test the speed you really get

The speed on your internet bill is the speed that reaches your modem. The speed that reaches your TV can be lower, mainly because of Wi-Fi.

1. Open a speed test such as Fast.com on the device you watch on, or on a phone held next to it.
2. Run the test in the evening, when home networks are busiest.
3. Compare the download number with the table above.

If the result near the TV is much lower than the result next to the router, the problem is your Wi-Fi signal, not your internet plan. Our guide to the [best router settings for streaming](/guides/router-settings-for-streaming/) covers the band, channel and QoS changes that help most.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does upload speed matter for streaming?

Very little. Watching video uses download speed. Upload speed matters for video calls and for sending files.

### Why does my picture drop to a lower quality in the evening?

More people in your home and your neighbourhood are online in the evening, so less speed is available. Most apps lower the picture quality to avoid pausing.

### Is 25 Mbps enough for a family?

It is enough for two HD streams with a little room. For more screens, or for 4K, plan for 50 Mbps or more.
