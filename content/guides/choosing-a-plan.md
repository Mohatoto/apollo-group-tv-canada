Every Apollo Group TV Canada plan includes the same service. The only differences are how long it lasts and how much each day costs. Longer plans cost less per day, and the 30-day plan is the lowest-risk way to try the service.

## The four plans side by side

Prices are in Canadian dollars, as listed on the website on 23 November 2026.

| Plan | Length | Price | Cost per day | Cost per 30 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 30 days | $24.99 | $0.83 | $24.99 |
| Growth | 90 days | $51.99 | $0.58 | $17.33 |
| Elite | 180 days | $89.99 | $0.50 | $15.00 |
| Professional | 365 days | $159.99 | $0.44 | $13.15 |

All plans are prepaid, with no long-term contract, and can be used on up to 5 devices per account.

## Which plan suits you

**Starter (30 days)** suits you if you are new to the service. You find out whether it works well on your devices and your internet connection before you commit to more.

**Growth (90 days)** suits you once the 30-day plan has worked for you and you want a lower price without paying for a full year.

**Elite (180 days)** suits households that already rely on the service and want to renew only twice a year.

**Professional (365 days)** has the lowest cost per day. It suits you if you are sure the service fits your home, since you pay for the whole year in advance.

## Things to check before choosing a long plan

1. **Test your setup first.** Use the service for a few weeks on the devices and rooms where you will really watch.
2. **Read the refund policy.** It explains when a refund is possible and how to ask for one.
3. **Remember how payment works.** Payment is by Interac e-Transfer, which cannot be reversed once it has been deposited. Paying for a short plan first limits what you have at stake.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do the plans renew automatically?

No. The site states that there are no automatic renewals. When a plan ends, you renew only if you want to continue.

### Can I move to a longer plan later?

Yes. The site says you can upgrade at any time. Contact support to arrange it.

### Do longer plans include more channels or devices?

No. The plans section lists the same features for every plan length.
